## Checks

- [ ] Open the archived session originally identified by `xrvkqmesu`, start its continuation, and record the time. Expected: the history preview leaves “Restoring”, the agent becomes ready, the archived conversation remains available, and a submitted continuation sends once. Confirm the new instance reaches “CLI spawned successfully” / “History restore complete” without a roughly two-minute delay before prompt assembly. Ordinary provider startup time is separate from instruction-loading time.
- [ ] Restore the Claude conversation rooted at `/Users/suas/work/communitytech`, then request another archived restore while it is starting. Expected: the first restore progresses to a usable session and the next queued restore also completes; neither remains behind an unrelated recursive filesystem scan.
- [ ] Check the restored provider/account/model and required project instructions. Expected: the archived selection and applicable instruction content are preserved. No provider fallback notice may be hidden or described as a successful native resume when the provider actually rejected the session.
- [ ] Warm a preview by typing, choose another account for the same provider and a different model, then continue. Expected: the same restored session keeps its conversation and chosen model, switches accounts through the normal account handoff, and sends once only after startup settles. Repeat with an account picked before the first restore; the requested account must match the actual ready session.
- [ ] Change the account again while its handoff is starting. Expected: preparation applies the latest choice before releasing the continuation. Choose a newer account after preparation while sending completes; that newer draft remains available for the next continuation.
- [ ] Remove an account override on a warmed session, then continue. Expected: it returns to the original routed account for that provider. Repeat with an account chosen before the initial restore, clearing it during restore and after warm restore but before the first continuation; it must return to the archived account (or legacy when the archive has no account). Change providers with an old account choice present, and clear that choice if the picker removes it; no account belonging to the archived provider is applied to the newly selected provider. Also warm an imported live preview, pick an account for its archived provider, and continue while another window's queued provider change finishes: preparation must use the settled live provider and avoid requesting the previous provider's account, without releasing work before readiness. Repeat with the archived-provider choice made only after that swap finishes; it must stay bound to the picker provider and must not be routed against the new live provider. Reverse the change back to the archived provider while a choice waits: the now-valid chosen account must apply before continuation. Repeat after an earlier successful preparation whose subsequent send or loop start failed without completing the preview: a new different-provider change must still finish before account routing. Repeat with a same-provider queued model change, where the chosen account must still apply. A local-model selection does not request a provider-account handoff.
- [ ] With a disposable archived conversation, use a provider account whose startup fails or is temporarily unavailable. Expected: the message remains unsent, the selected account/model draft remains visible, and a later retry succeeds after the account becomes usable. A stalled restore or account handoff reports a retryable error after approximately 30 seconds; a late completed restore is reused on retry.
- [ ] After an account handoff times out, clear it or choose another account/model/provider while the old handoff is still pending, then retry. Expected: the continuation remains unsent until the earlier backend write finishes and the latest choice is confirmed. Repeat when the account request queued behind a busy session, then began applying; the pending indicator remains visible until actual completion and the continuation is still blocked. A later backend completion must never undo the latest confirmed choice.
- [ ] Reopen or reconnect the renderer while restored sessions are present. Expected: initial session loading and authoritative resynchronization show the actual account and routing information consistently with subsequent live updates.
- [ ] Reopen an imported archived session while its live runtime is still starting, leave account/model choices unchanged, and submit a loop. Expected: the loop form and unsent draft remain until startup succeeds; no provider iteration starts early. Successful startup starts the loop once. Startup failure or a roughly30-second preparation timeout retains the loop draft and reports a useful retryable error; retry uses the existing restored instance. Also change an account/model choice while startup is held: the latest choice must be confirmed before starting the loop.
- [ ] With a disposable restored Claude conversation awaiting permission, queue a YOLO toggle and confirm the currently selected account, then approve the deferred tool with YOLO enabled. Expected: the account request finishes, the pending indicator clears, and continuation remains usable without an unnecessary second respawn. With another model/account also pending, approval must preserve and finish that remaining change. Stop or fail a session while an account change is queued: its pending indicator must clear and continuation must remain unsent with a useful error.
- [ ] Reconnect the renderer while an account change is pending, reopen the same imported archived session, choose its original account, then continue. Expected: the pending change remains visible, the message waits through its actual completion and the original-account reset, and the later write cannot undo the chosen account. Repeat without selecting an account or model: it must still wait for the existing pending change and retain its resulting runtime. Timeout/failure keeps the message unsent and retryable. Also finish a busy loop while an account change is waiting; that single release must allow the queued change to complete without requiring another user message or status transition. Queue another account change while the first is finishing, then begin a new loop or turn: the newer pending change must remain visible and a reconnect must wait for it, for both respawn and supported in-place account changes.
- [ ] While an unchanged selected model is still starting, choose a newer recovery model before the first runtime reports an error; repeat with a newer account for the same model. Expected: preparation attempts the latest valid choice, confirms successful model/provider/account readiness, and retains unsent work if recovery fails. A model change already in flight must finish before another target replaces it. Change from model A to B and back to A while B is still applying: A must receive its own final confirmation. A choice edited during submission can keep the draft for review and resubmission under the existing composer safeguard; do not treat that safeguard as an automatic send failure.
- [ ] During a queued model/account/provider change, watch the pending indicator while ordinary status updates arrive, then watch it clear after completion or stopping the session. Expected: status-only updates cannot hide a real pending target, undo an explicit clear or revert the confirmed provider. Repeat after reconnecting the renderer; the displayed state must agree with the actual ready session.
- [ ] Begin an account change on a disposable idle session, then stop/remove that session while its account change is still finishing. Repeat with an account change queued behind a busy turn. Expected: the pending indicator clears, the finished request reports that the session is unavailable, and no unsent continuation is released by a late success. A removed or replacement session must not inherit the old account request, overwrite its own pending model/account choice or receive the old operation's failure in its transcript, including when that operation rejects instead of returning a result. Verify both a provider requiring restart and one supporting an in-place account change when available.
